Design Philosophy
Two Principles
01
AI as mirror, not replacement.
The AI does not write your story. It reflects your choices with enough structure that the reflection holds. Your creativity. Your decisions. The AI keeps the world consistent.
02
Solo companion, not AM replacement.
Human Archive Masters are better. The app exists for Tuesday night when your group chat is dead and your AM has a baby. The app fills the gap. It is not the table.
"Neurodivergence is not a debuff to balance a buff. It is a different operating system that runs different software. Some of that software is extraordinary. The cost is that the hardware requirements are non-standard and the world only sells standard parts."
