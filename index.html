A note from the bookkeeper

You do the work. I keep the record.

I'm not an accountant, I'm a storyteller. What I do is help people tell government and financial institutions their story in the way that communicates their true life in the most fiscally productive way. I'm not telling the story — I'm hearing the story and recording it in a way that helps you tell the story to the institutions who will not take as much time to listen.

I.

Who this page is for

You run a food truck, a restaurant, a bar, a yoga studio, a dispensary. You sell at a farmers market, on Etsy, through Square or PayPal or Venmo. You are making or growing things — food, flowers, herbs, jewelry, candles, pottery, bread. You are holding rooms for people who came to be there. Your work is embodied, and the money moves while you are doing the thing. You want it tracked without having to stop being in your life to do it.

I read bank statements, POS exports, and payment processor reports and I sort them into categories the government and your future self will need. At the end of an engagement I return a Google Sheets booklet you can use to self-file your taxes, or hand to whoever files for you, or keep as the clean record your work deserves.
